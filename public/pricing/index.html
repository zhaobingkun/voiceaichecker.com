Free-first policy

The main detector remains free. Pro at $9.90 per month includes up to 20 checks per day and is meant to cover heavier usage, provider costs, and abuse-resistant quota handling. A user should be able to understand the result and the tool limitations before seeing any payment prompt.

Payments

Pro checkout is handled by Creem. The checkout page shows the $9.90 monthly price, renewal terms, cancellation path, and refund policy before purchase.

Important limitation

AI voice detection is probabilistic. A paid check would not guarantee a more certain result, a legal finding, or a forensic conclusion. Results should be reviewed with audio context, source evidence, and direct confirmation when stakes are high.
